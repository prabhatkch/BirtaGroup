About the Group

One Group.
Many Horizons.

Founded on the belief that sustainable growth is always collective, BIRTA is a multi-sector holding group building enduring enterprises across industries — united by shared vision, disciplined capital, and long-term thinking.

Headquartered in Nepal and expanding across the region, we operate a diverse portfolio of companies, each leading in its sector while sharing the values and ambition that define the BIRTA identity.
